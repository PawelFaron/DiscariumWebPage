# Product wording

Suggested homepage / promotional language:

> Your collection. A private performance.
>
> Lossless audio, original-quality video and the disc experience, brought together on Apple TV. Built for music lovers, audiophiles and the system you’ve made your own.

Promotional text (under Apple's 170-character limit):

> Your collection. A private performance. Lossless albums, surround sound, concerts and compatible Blu-ray and DVD menus. On Apple TV.

This promotional text matches the published app store copy. The website lists features by version in `updates.html`, without publishing review or approval statuses.

Paragraph for a store description:

> Bring your own collection to the big screen. Discarium plays supported Blu-ray, DVD-Audio and DVD-Video copies directly on Apple TV, as folders or ISO images. Browse programs, chapters and soundtracks, and navigate compatible original Blu-ray HDMV, BD-J and DVD-Video menus with your remote. Enjoy lossless audio and supported video without additional lossy re-encoding by the app.

Describe specific playback features: lossless audio decoded to PCM, original-resolution video, compatible Blu-ray HDMV/BD-J and DVD-Video menus. Keep source-format information distinct from the output reported by Apple TV.

Current public version: **1.3**, checked with Apple’s lookup API on 19 October 2026.
Its description includes CUE albums and SMB, NFS, FTP, FTPES, FTPS, SFTP, WebDAV
and UPnP/DLNA connections. MilkDrop, the redesigned speaker meters and complete
queue history are described only in the clearly labeled development preview.
Do not advertise those additions as part of version 1.3 until a new store version
containing them is verified.
