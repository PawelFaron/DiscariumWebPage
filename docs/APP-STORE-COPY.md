# Product wording

Suggested homepage / promotional language:

> Your collection. A private performance.
>
> Lossless audio, original-quality video and the disc experience, brought together on Apple TV. Built for music lovers, audiophiles and the system you’ve made your own.

Promotional text (under Apple's 170-character limit):

> Your collection. A private performance. Lossless albums, surround sound, concerts and compatible Blu-ray and DVD menus. On Apple TV.

This promotional text matches the version 1.1 (28) store copy. The website lists features by version in `updates.html`, without publishing review or approval statuses.

Paragraph for a store description:

> Bring your own collection to the big screen. Discarium plays supported Blu-ray, DVD-Audio and DVD-Video copies directly on Apple TV, as folders or ISO images. Browse programs, chapters and soundtracks, and navigate compatible original Blu-ray HDMV and DVD-Video menus with your remote. Enjoy lossless audio and supported video without additional lossy re-encoding by the app.

Describe specific playback features: lossless audio decoded to PCM, original-resolution video, Blu-ray HDMV menus and DVD-Video menus. Keep source-format information distinct from the output reported by Apple TV.
