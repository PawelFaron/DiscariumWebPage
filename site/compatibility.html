The whole disc experience.

Play supported disc copies directly on Apple TV. Browse programs and chapters, choose a soundtrack and navigate original menus with your remote.

Discs and original menus
Format Features
Blu-ray folders & ISO Unencrypted disc copies, with programs, chapters and audio-track selection.
Blu-ray HDMV menus Compatible original interactive menus, controlled with the remote.
DVD-Video folders & ISO Unencrypted disc copies, titles, chapters and compatible original DVD menus.
DVD-Audio Audio programs and tracks, including supported high-resolution stereo and multichannel recordings.

See each disc’s size and available sound versions before you play. Download a supported disc copy for local playback.

Coming in 1.1: chapter selection during DVD and supported Blu-ray playback, improved subtitle seeking, and network read-ahead using your video buffering setting.

Lossless audio. In stereo and surround.

Audio formats
Format Playback
FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, WavPack Lossless audio, including high-resolution source files up to 24-bit/192 kHz.
Stereo, 5.1 & 7.1 Supported discrete channel layouts for your hi-fi or surround system.
LPCM & DVD-Audio MLP Supported channel-based audio decoded to PCM.
DTS-HD Master Audio & Dolby TrueHD Lossless soundtrack decoding to multichannel PCM.
MP3, AAC, Dolby Digital & DTS Playback of these common compressed audio formats.
Gapless Continuous playback between compatible tracks with matching formats on the supported playback path.

Volume normalization and crossfade are off by default. Playback settings let you choose how to listen.

Original resolution. Every scene.

Play supported video at its source resolution, without additional lossy video re-encoding by the app.

Video and playback features
Format or feature Playback
MKV and supported video files Direct playback of compatible video and audio streams.
AVC / H.264, HEVC / H.265 & MPEG-2 Supported decoding, including compatible 4K HEVC and disc video.
4K & HDR10 Original-resolution decoding and supported HDR10 on compatible equipment.
Subtitles Supported embedded and sidecar subtitles. Text size, position, color, timing and encoding controls.
Chapters Available embedded chapters, chapter selection and markers on the progress bar.
Resume & buffering Saved video position and audio/subtitle choices, plus a separate buffering setting for video.

See what reaches your system.

Sound details shows the codec, bit depth, sample rate and channel layout alongside the audio output reported by Apple TV. Video details shows resolution, frame rate and available HDR information.

Connect your receiver over HDMI and use the speaker test to check channel mapping. The final output format depends on tvOS, Apple TV and your connected equipment.

Coming in 1.1: 7.1 recordings can be mixed to a 5.1 HDMI output, with a visible Mixed to 5.1 indicator. An available eight-channel output keeps all 7.1 channels.

Your media, where you keep it.

Connect SMB network drives and UPnP/DLNA media servers. Browse your chosen locations and completed downloads on Apple TV.

Use Test connection to check access and Test speed to measure a file transfer. Download albums and supported disc copies without conversion, with progress, transfer speed, time remaining and pause/resume controls.
