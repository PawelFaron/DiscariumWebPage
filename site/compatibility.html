The disc experience.

Discarium reads supported disc copies directly on Apple TV. A server supplies files; it does not need to convert them into a streaming version first.

Discs and original menus
Source Current support
Blu-ray folders & ISO Supported unencrypted copies. Programs, chapters and audio-track selection.
Blu-ray HDMV menus Compatible original interactive menus, controlled with the remote. Authored features and disc compatibility vary.
DVD-Video folders & ISO Supported titles, chapters and compatible original DVD menus.
DVD-Audio Supported audio programs and tracks. Universal original DVD-Audio menu navigation is not promised.
Blu-ray BD-J menus Not included in the current release. These Java-based menus differ from HDMV menus.
Encrypted discs Not supported. Discarium does not remove AACS, BD+ or DVD encryption.

Use complete disc copies that you are entitled to access. Missing files, unusual authoring, secondary streams or publisher-dependent features may prevent a disc from working as expected.

Lossless, from the source.

Audio formats
Format Playback
FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, WavPack Supported lossless sources, including high-resolution recordings up to 24-bit/192 kHz.
Stereo, 5.1 & 7.1 Supported discrete channel layouts. Available output channels depend on the playback path and connected equipment.
LPCM & DVD-Audio MLP Supported channel-based audio decoded to PCM.
DTS-HD Master Audio & Dolby TrueHD Supported decoding to multichannel PCM. This does not preserve Atmos or DTS:X object output.
MP3, AAC, Dolby Digital & DTS Supported common lossy audio formats. Decoding cannot restore information removed by the original compression.
Gapless Playback between compatible tracks with matching formats on the supported playback path.

Volume normalization and crossfade are off by default. Optional processing changes the sound; check Sound details and your playback settings when fidelity is the priority.

The picture, without another encode.

Most films and disc video are already compressed. “Without re-encoding” means Discarium does not add another lossy video encode to supported playback; it does not turn a compressed film into a lossless original.

Video and display features
Feature Current support
MKV and other supported video files Direct playback of compatible video/audio streams. A container name alone does not guarantee every codec or feature inside it.
AVC / H.264, HEVC / H.265 & MPEG-2 Supported decoding, including compatible 4K HEVC and disc video.
Resolution & HDR10 Original-resolution decoding with supported HDR10 on compatible equipment. Display output depends on tvOS and the HDMI chain.
Dolby Vision disc video Full Dolby Vision disc playback is not supported. Do not assume a Dolby Vision source label guarantees Dolby Vision output.
Subtitles & chapters Supported embedded and sidecar subtitles, text appearance/encoding controls, available embedded chapters and chapter markers.
Resume & buffering Saved video position and audio/subtitle choices. A separate buffering setting for video.

Source quality. Output clarity.

A file’s format, the decoded signal and the HDMI output are three different things. Discarium shows what it knows about the source and the output reported by tvOS.

  • No universal bit-perfect guarantee. tvOS can change sample rate, channel count or processing. Matching reported values do not measure every sample at the receiver.
  • PCM is a valid lossless audio output. Decoding a supported lossless codec to PCM preserves its channel-based audio; it does not require the receiver to display the original codec name.
  • Surround channels and sound objects differ. TrueHD Atmos and DTS:X object output are not supported in this release.
  • Video still goes through a display pipeline. Frame-rate matching, color conversion, deinterlacing, tone mapping and television settings can affect the result even without video re-encoding.

Connect Apple TV over HDMI, check Sound details and Video details, and use your receiver and TV’s information displays to confirm the actual signal.

Your media, where you keep it.

Connect SMB network drives and UPnP/DLNA media servers. Browse supported local media and completed downloads on Apple TV. Other network protocols are not advertised as supported.

A UPnP/DLNA server chooses the resources it exposes and may offer a converted version. For original files, configure the server accordingly or access the originals through SMB.

Offline albums and supported disc copies are saved without conversion. tvOS can reclaim this storage, so keep your source collection elsewhere.
