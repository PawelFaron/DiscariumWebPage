Who is responsible.

Discarium is developed by Pawel Faron. For privacy questions or requests, contact:

discarium@pisz.to

The app does not require a Discarium account. It has no ads, tracking SDK or separate service that receives your media library, playback history or network-drive passwords.

Information on your device.

To provide browsing and playback, Discarium stores information locally on Apple TV, including:

  • Media locations, filenames, metadata, artwork and the library index.
  • Collections, playlists, favorites, tags, ratings and notes.
  • Playback history, saved video positions and audio/subtitle selections.
  • Settings, saved server details, and download progress and files.

Network-drive passwords are stored in Apple’s Keychain. Other library information is stored in the app’s local data. Discarium reads your chosen media sources and does not edit the original files on your server.

Connections you choose.

Discarium connects to the SMB drives and UPnP/DLNA servers you select. Discovering servers sends requests on your network. Browsing, reading artwork, playback and downloading send the requests and authentication information required by your chosen server.

A media server can supply links to artwork or media on another server. Those servers receive the requests, including the network address from which they arrive, and may retain logs under their own policies. Discarium does not route these requests through the developer’s servers.

These are user-selected media services, not developer advertising or analytics partners. Choose servers you trust and review their data practices. Discarium does not sell or share your library with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention, deletion and your choices.

Local library information and playback preferences remain on your Apple TV while the app retains its data; there is no developer-hosted copy of this information to delete. Cache files and downloads can be removed by tvOS when storage is needed.

  • Downloads: remove a download in Settings → Downloads to delete its local files, including partial files.
  • Connections: remove a saved connection in Settings → Connections to remove its saved configuration and associated collection locations. To revoke server access completely, revoke or change the credential on the server as well. Apple’s Keychain can retain credentials across app installations.
  • Local network permission: revoke access in Apple TV Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Network browsing and playback will then be unavailable.
  • App data: deleting Discarium removes its local app data and downloaded files. Keychain items and data retained by Apple or your own server may remain under their respective controls.

Discarium has no app account to delete. Removing a collection or a download does not delete your original media from the server.

When you contact us or use TestFlight.

If you email support, the developer receives the address you send from, your message and any attachments you choose to include. These are used to answer your request and investigate the issue, not for advertising. Avoid including credentials or private media.

Support correspondence is kept while the request is being handled and for up to 12 months after the last exchange, unless a longer period is needed for an ongoing dispute or a legal obligation. You can ask for deletion earlier. Email delivery providers process correspondence to provide their service.

Apple handles TestFlight installation information, diagnostics, crash reports and feedback under its own terms and privacy policy. Apple may share reports and feedback with the developer. Discarium has no additional crash-reporting or usage-analytics service. Retention of Apple-hosted TestFlight data is controlled by Apple.

If you post to a public GitHub issue, the information you submit is public and processed by GitHub. Please use email for private requests.

When you visit this website.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use browser storage, load third-party fonts or embed tracking services. Images, styles and scripts are served with the site.

GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. GitHub controls that hosting data and its retention. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection.

Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices. Opening an email link uses your chosen email application. There is no contact form that uploads data through this website.

Questions and privacy rights.

Contact Pawel Faron at the address below to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information held through support. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, or complain to your data protection authority.

discarium@pisz.to

Support messages are handled to respond to your request and, where applicable, on the basis of legitimate interests in maintaining and supporting the app. The developer cannot directly access or delete files stored only on your Apple TV or records controlled by your own server.
