1.1 — Discs, sound and playback.

More reliable disc playback, clearer sound information and easier navigation. Submitted to Apple; available on the App Store after approval.

Surround sound that fits your system.

Play 7.1 recordings on a 5.1 HDMI system. Discarium combines the surround channels and shows Mixed to 5.1 in the playback controls. An available eight-channel output keeps all 7.1 channels. Sound details shows the source, output and any processing.

More reliable disc sound.

Improved TrueHD and DTS-HD detection on Blu-ray, including short clips and intros. Incomplete audio details saved during an earlier scan are refreshed when the disc is opened.

Your next chapter, a click away.

Choose chapters while watching a DVD or supported Blu-ray. Seek with the remote, pause and continue. Blu-ray subtitles are restored with their timing after seeking.

Ahead of the next scene.

Disc video uses the video buffering setting to read ahead during network playback. Choose a larger buffer to help with brief network interruptions.

A clearer view of your videos.

Video collections show frame previews. Press Up from the playback buttons to reach the timeline, then Up again to hide the controls. Multichannel PCM playback in MKV is corrected, including 24-bit recordings.

The disc opening button is now named Open Disc.

Available on the App Store8 October 2026

1.0 — Your collection. A private performance.

Discarium’s first release brings your discs, music and concert films to Apple TV, in a clean interface designed for the sofa.

  • Lossless audio, high-resolution source files up to 24-bit/192 kHz, stereo and supported multichannel recordings.
  • Blu-ray, DVD-Video and DVD-Audio copies in folders or ISO images, with compatible original HDMV and DVD menus.
  • Original-resolution video, compatible 4K and HDR10, subtitles, embedded chapters and saved video positions.
  • SMB drives, UPnP/DLNA music servers, collections, playlists and downloads for local playback.
  • Sound details, speaker tests, album artwork and gentle visualizations.
  • An interface in English, Polish, German, French, Italian and Spanish.

Requires Apple TV with tvOS 27 or later and your own media. Original demo music is included. Explore supported formats or set up your collection.
