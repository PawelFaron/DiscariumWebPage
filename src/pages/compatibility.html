The whole disc experience.

Play supported disc copies directly on Apple TV. Browse programs and chapters, choose a soundtrack and navigate original menus with your remote.

Discs and original menus
Format Features
Blu-ray folders & ISO Unencrypted disc copies, with programs, chapters and audio-track selection.
Blu-ray HDMV & BD-J menus Compatible original interactive menus, including BD-J. Select languages, programs, scenes and soundtracks with your remote.
DVD-Video folders & ISO Unencrypted disc copies, titles, chapters and compatible original DVD menus.
DVD-Audio Audio programs and tracks, including supported high-resolution stereo and multichannel recordings.

See each disc’s size, available sound versions and embedded cover before you play. Artwork can come from Blu-ray metadata or DVD jacket pictures. Download a supported disc copy for local playback.

Choose chapters during DVD and Blu-ray playback, with chapter markers on the timeline. Video buffering reads ahead during network playback. Faster Blu-ray seeking and subtitle synchronization after network interruptions are included in version 1.3.

Lossless audio. In stereo and surround.

Volume normalization and crossfade are off by default. Playback settings let you choose how to listen.

See what reaches your system.

Sound details shows the codec, bit depth, sample rate and channel layout alongside the audio output reported by Apple TV. Video details shows resolution, frame rate and available HDR information.

Connect your receiver over HDMI and use the speaker test to check channel mapping. The final output format depends on tvOS, Apple TV and your connected equipment.

7.1 recordings can be mixed to a 5.1 HDMI output, with a visible Mixed to 5.1 indicator. An available eight-channel output keeps all 7.1 channels.

Your media, where you keep it.

Connect network drives and servers over SMB, NFSv3, FTP, FTPES, FTPS, SFTP or WebDAV. WebDAV supports HTTP and HTTPS; FTPES uses explicit TLS and FTPS uses implicit TLS. Browse audio, video and disc locations from each saved connection. You can also browse UPnP/DLNA music servers.

Use Test connection while setting up a server and Test speed to measure a file transfer. Advanced options include server ports and protocol-specific settings. SFTP remembers the server identity you accept; passwords are stored in Apple’s Keychain.

Set up your collection ↗

Organized around your collection.

  • Separate Discs, Audio and Video sections, with named collections and locations managed in Settings.
  • Browse audio by album, artist, genre or folder. Search as you type, build playlists, and keep personal tags, ratings and notes.
  • Sort by name, size or date where available. Video collections show frame previews and dates added; folder views keep the original names.
  • Open file information, rename items or delete files and folders from the long-press menu when the source permits changes.
  • Use the playback queue, shuffle and repeat. Choose album artwork, visualizations or a clear fullscreen view, with optional control auto-hide.
  • English, Polish, German, French, Italian and Spanish interfaces.

Your next listen, already here.

Save albums and supported disc copies without conversion. Follow transfer speed, progress and estimated time remaining. Pause, resume or remove complete and partial downloads. CUE tracks share one downloaded audio image instead of duplicating it for each song.

Disc sizes and available storage use matching units. Completed downloads play without the server. tvOS manages this storage and may reclaim cached media when space is needed.
