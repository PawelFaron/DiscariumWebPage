Your first evening with Discarium.

  1. Get Discarium.

    Install Discarium from the App Store on Apple TV. Start with the included demo or connect your own collection.

  2. Try the demo.

    Open Audio → Try Demo Library. The included music and artwork are original, and work without a server or app account.

  3. Connect your collection.

    Open Settings → Connections → Add connection. Enter your SMB server address, username and password. Save it and use Test connection to check access.

  4. Choose what to include.

    Go to Settings → Collections and choose Discs, Audio or Video. Add locations from your saved connection. For discs, select a single disc or a folder to scan. Browse and play from the main menu.

Requires Apple TV running tvOS 27 or later. The interface is available in English, Polish, German, French, Italian and Spanish, following Apple TV’s language preferences. Relaunch the app after changing the language.

Network & downloads

My network drive does not connect.

Check that Apple TV can reach the server. Confirm its current address, share permissions, username and password. On Apple TV, allow Discarium access to the local network in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Start with automatic SMB version selection and the server’s usual port. If you are connecting from outside your home, your network must provide a route to the server, for example through a VPN.

Playback is slow, but the server is connected.

Open the saved connection and choose Test speed. Select a large media file on the same drive. A disc or high-bitrate film needs sustained throughput, not just a successful connection. Try wired Ethernet and compare with a completed local download.

Video has its own buffering setting. Increasing it may help with brief interruptions. From version 1.1, disc video uses the same setting. A wired connection is a good starting point for large video files.

A download stopped. Can I resume it?

Open Settings → Downloads, restore the network connection and resume the transfer. Progress, transfer speed and estimated time remaining appear while data is being downloaded. Keep Discarium open during transfers.

Remove a download from this screen to delete its downloaded files, including partial files. Deleting a local download does not delete the source on your server.

Why did a downloaded album or disc disappear?

tvOS can remove cached media when it needs space. Offline copies on Apple TV should not be your only copy. Keep the originals on your own drive or server and download them again if needed.

Picture & sound

How should I connect my receiver?

Connect Apple TV by HDMI to an input on your receiver, then connect the receiver to the TV. Start with Apple TV’s Audio Format set to Auto. Check your receiver’s input and speaker configuration.

Use Discarium’s speaker test to check channel mapping. During playback, open Sound details and compare the source with the output reported by tvOS. Confirm what arrives on the receiver’s information screen.

Where can I check the audio quality?

Open Sound details during playback to see the codec, bit depth, sample rate and channels. Discarium shows the source alongside the output reported by Apple TV.

High-resolution source support goes up to 24-bit/192 kHz. The HDMI output format is determined by tvOS and your connected equipment.

How are Dolby TrueHD and DTS-HD Master Audio played?

Discarium decodes these lossless soundtracks to multichannel PCM for your HDMI receiver. Sound details shows the recording’s format, channel layout and reported output.

PCM is the decoded sound. Lossless decoding preserves the soundtrack’s samples; the final channel count and sample rate depend on the output available to Apple TV.

What does “Mixed to 5.1” mean?

From version 1.1, Discarium can play a 7.1 recording on an HDMI output that provides 5.1. The extra surround channels are combined with the 5.1 surround channels. A small Mixed to 5.1 indicator appears in the video controls.

This changes the channel mix to fit your system. When tvOS provides an eight-channel output, Discarium keeps all 7.1 channels. Open Sound details to compare the recording and output.

How do I use original disc menus?

Open a disc and choose Open Disc (Open disc menu in version 1.0). Use your remote to navigate compatible Blu-ray HDMV and DVD-Video menus.

You can also browse the disc’s programs, chapters and soundtracks from its detail screen. See supported disc formats.

How do I play 4K and HDR10 video?

Use compatible Apple TV, receiver, display and HDMI connections. Discarium plays supported 4K HEVC and HDR10 video at the source resolution, without additional video re-encoding.

Open Video details to see the resolution, frame rate and available HDR information. Check your television’s information display for the active picture mode.

Playback & subtitles

How do I choose a chapter while a disc is playing?

From version 1.1, show the playback controls and choose Chapters. Select a chapter to jump there. This works during DVD and supported Blu-ray playback when the current program provides chapters.

How do I reach the video timeline with the remote?

In version 1.1, press Up from the playback buttons to select the timeline. Use Left and Right to seek. Press Down to return to Play/Pause, or Up again to hide the controls.

How do I choose Blu-ray subtitles?

Choose an available subtitle track in the playback controls. Blu-ray PGS subtitles are stored as pictures, so accented characters are part of the image and need no text-encoding setting. Version 1.1 improves their restoration and timing after seeking.

Subtitles show incorrect accents or Polish characters.

Open Settings → Subtitles. UTF-8 is the best starting point for modern text subtitles. For an older subtitle file, select its original encoding, such as Windows-1250 for many Central European subtitles.

For text subtitles, you can also adjust size, position, color and timing.

Can I resume a video and choose chapters?

Discarium remembers video playback position and audio/subtitle choices locally on Apple TV. When a saved position is available, you can continue or start again. Embedded chapters appear when the file provides them.

This history is stored on the device, not in a Discarium cloud account, and may be lost if the app or its data is removed.

Can I change the visualization?

Choose an animation from the music player or open Settings → Animations. Adjust movement to suit the room. Discarium remembers your selection, and you can choose artwork or hide the controls for a quieter view.
